Privacy Policy

Keep Notes · last updated August 3, 2026

1. Summary

Keep Notes (“the app”) is a note-taking application published by Cool App Helpdesk. It lets you create notes, checklists, labels, and reminders.

  • Your notes, checklists, labels, colors, and reminders are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • The app contains non-intrusive advertising via Google AdMob, with a Google consent form (UMP) in the EEA/UK.
  • Link previews request the pasted URL only from that website, not from us.

2. Data we collect

The app itself does not collect personal data. However, the Google AdMob SDK integrated in the app may collect certain information:

5. Network activity and third parties

Notes are stored locally and never leave your device. Two features use the network:

  • Link previews — when you paste a URL into a note, the app requests that web page directly from its server to extract a title, description, and image. The URL is sent only to that website, not to us.
  • Google AdMob — serves advertising and may collect device information, advertising IDs, and IP addresses, managed by Google under its Privacy Policy. In the EEA/UK a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form lets you choose how your data is used for ads.

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no social or attribution SDKs beyond AdMob.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data you create in Keep Notes remains on your device until you delete it inside the app, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Keep Notes → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
